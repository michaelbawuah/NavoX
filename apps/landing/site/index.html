Fair questions

Does it work at my school?

Yes, any college. Add your classes by talking, importing your schedule file, or snapping a photo. Canvas, Blackboard, Moodle and Brightspace calendar feeds can connect too.

Will it send emails or change my calendar on its own?

Never. NavoX shows exactly what it will do and waits for your yes. Every calendar change comes with Undo.

Does my school or professor see my data?

No. Your data is never shared with your school, professors or advertisers, and never sold.

Will it just do my homework?

It teaches step by step on the Board, checks your work line by line, and respects your course’s AI policy.
