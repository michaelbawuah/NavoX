# News availability for new accounts

The operator catalog's Perigon templates reference an owner-scoped connection.
A template is not read authority for another signed-in account. The source picker
now lists an API source only after the existing connector check passes for the
current user and workspace. Activation, ingestion, story reads and rights checks
keep their existing ownership boundaries.

Accounts with no permitted sources see a plain account-availability message and
an Ask NavoX link. Empty category and saved-story states remain distinct. A source
404 no longer says a story disappeared. Unavailable source management, category
and News-chat controls are hidden; no private owner feed is copied.

## Separate broad News provisioning blocker

The existing Perigon free profile is a personal/integration trial. Its current
[Cloud Service Agreement](https://perigon.io/CSA.pdf), retrieved 2026-10-04 UTC,
states the free plan is not intended for ongoing production use and retains
underlying third-party content rights. An upgraded service plan alone does not
establish permission for publisher photographs or generated detailed summaries.
The current metadata-only rights and one-day retention have not changed.

The BBC's official [feed catalog](https://support.bbc.co.uk/platform/feeds/NewsFeeds.htm)
links feed-specific terms. Its official
[Terms PDF](https://downloads.bbc.co.uk/usingthebbc/bbc_terms_of_use_31March2022english.pdf)
(version 1.72, March 2022), retrieved on the same date, distinguishes personal RSS
display from business RSS/metadata use requiring permission. Current direct
terms pages could not be fetched, so this is not treated as a fresh commercial
license. No BBC source is provisioned by this repair.

The existing reviewed USGS public configuration remains unchanged and inactive
for fresh accounts. It covers earthquake science data only, with attribution,
and is not substituted for the requested broad general News feed. No new source,
image or summary rights, provider grant, subscription, or paid account has been
installed. A permitted production feed and applicable publisher content rights
remain the separate provisioning step.
