## Scope decisions

- The written **90-minute maximum** wins over the two-hour example blocks in the
  Planner artwork. Planning, Move and placement respect the saved maximum,
  which cannot exceed 90 minutes. Long work is split into shorter blocks.
- Classes, Planner and Inbox redesigns are in this revised M2 scope even though
  the older milestone schedule placed those pages later. The source explicitly
  allows the **full Settings redesign to finish in milestone 5**.
- Plans, routine/alarm changes and sends need exact review and approval.
  Reading a source, interpreting speech or preparing a draft does not approve a
  write. Mail read permission does not authorize sending; external calendar
  writes remain separately consented and gated.
- Preserve Vox playback, interruption, reconnection, private transcript boundaries
  and usage accounting. A visual call state is not real-audio evidence.
  Picovoice approval remains an external dependency.
- Sample student facts and messages belong only in clearly labelled QA data.
  Engineering fixtures do not count as real student usage.

### Vox task handoff correction

The live call check on `3244a91` exposed a real failure after task review appeared:
the model narrated enough detail to exceed the existing 30-second aggregate
audio limit, causing reconnect before the next message. Content-free diagnostics
on `74711c0` identified the audio-bound rejection; no provider text or credentials
were logged. Those earlier calls are not accepted continuity passes.

The task and remaining-work instructions now request a handoff of at most 45
spoken words, and the tools supply the affected-block count without individual
block details to the model. The complete browser review still retrieves every block with the same
payload and preview hash, and still requires explicit approval. Regression
cases cover 1, 9 and 13 affected blocks for both task operations. The 30-second
audio limit and no-replay/fallback rules remain unchanged. The final live check
requires complete transcript checkpoints before navigation, one media admission,
unchanged saved tasks/courses/blocks/routine, clean hangup and transcript expiry.

## Planning, recovery and approval

Planning excludes saved classes/shifts, authorized calendar busy time, sleep,
protected routine intervals, travel and breaks. It spreads work, reserves about
15% capacity and exposes work that cannot fit. Missing calendar coverage or
unreviewed routine assumptions cannot become a verified free-time claim.

Replan review lists every future block it replaces, including affected blocks
outside the new horizon. Move, progress and task completion revalidate the exact
preview on the server. Changed owner/source/revision/routine/calendar facts
invalidate stale approval. Completing a task removes its remaining work without
fabricating study minutes.

### Exact-window placement and recovery

Today carries the chosen interval in an owner-bound, ten-minute handoff. Planner
keeps it through task selection or creation. Suitable existing blocks use Move;
unallocated effort uses an exact placement preview. The server checks the
current session, task/source, calendar, saved zone/routine, same-day fit,
deadline, breaks, 90-minute cap and daily plus remaining pre-deadline buffer.

If task creation retired an earlier plan, review shows remaining unplanned work
and preserved blocks. It requires a separate recovery acknowledgment and
**Start this reviewed plan** approval. Remaining work stays visible afterward;
retired blocks never revive. Stale surviving blocks require full Replan while
retaining the selected window. Placement does not silently move other blocks,
change alarms or create external events.

Source: [placement service](../../services/api/navox/student_planning/placement.py),
[placement review](../../apps/web/src/components/study-window-placement.tsx),
[planner](../../services/api/navox/student_planning/planner.py).

### Shared routine and alarm intent

Class-day setup derives actual upcoming class weekdays, including weekend
classes. Non-class days use their separately chosen wake time. Saved per-day
wake/sleep/travel and smart-alarm boundaries feed planning and availability.
Routine review shows old/new alarms and every study block it retires; alarm-only
changes preserve work when planning is unaffected. Vox uses the same review for
wake time, enable/disable and skip-tomorrow commands. Unknown walking time or
ambiguous clock changes cannot produce a confident smart-alarm approval.

## Inbox boundaries and while-open checks

### Read permission, source capture and bounded counts

Outlook setup requires `read_enabled` plus `CONNECTED` or `DEGRADED`; identity
alone does not complete the email step. Calendar completion requires the
purpose-bound student-calendar grant. Outlook mail does not enable Outlook
calendar availability, and read consent does not authorize Send.

Outlook discovery checks one page of at most **50 recent Inbox messages**.
Today/navigation check at most **three** readable Outlook mailboxes per refresh.
Partial or failed coverage is disclosed. Pending/sent sources leave Needs reply.
Unsupported shapes, including multiple Reply-To recipients, can be omitted;
Outlook remains the complete mailbox view.

Gmail/Outlook task capture uses one selected, currently readable message. Shared
review exposes evidence and requires the title, course/deadline decisions,
effort, revision and source hash. Preparation does not save a task or send mail.
Accepted copies retain provenance; changed/revoked source access blocks planning
until resolved. Forget/reset removes associated derived copies under the
existing cleanup contract.

### Meeting source → review → availability → draft

The already-read email supplies a bounded conservative meeting interpretation.
Review shows its exact passage, sent time, saved zone and assumptions. Task
deadlines are not meeting proposals. Ambiguous year/date/AM-PM, foreign zone,
DST, multiple options, quoted changes or truncation require correction; a
missing duration is disclosed for review.

Only explicit source/time review enables a fresh routine/classes/study/calendar
check, with ten-minute meeting buffers. A clash or unconfirmed result is shown
honestly, with at most two checked alternatives. Source, owner, time or access
changes retire the result; UI availability expires within 60 seconds. Using an
option fills reply instructions and clears prior send approval. It creates no
calendar event and sends no message.

### Send proof, Waiting and privacy

Outlook `202 Accepted` is not sent confirmation. Success requires the exact sent
Message-ID, matching MIME fingerprint and verified receipt. That proves the Sent
record, not recipient delivery. Uncertain results offer read-only **Check
outcome**, never automatic resend. Approval lasts 15 minutes; private reply
content has a seven-day availability limit and is cleared on disconnect.
Content-free execution fences remain to prevent duplicate sends after reconnect.

Reply tracking starts after exact send proof. Each check reads at most **50 later
metadata records** in that mailbox/conversation. Matching requires actual later
provider time, exact reviewed recipient/From/sender, the owned mailbox and a
References/In-Reply-To link to the outgoing message. Drafts, self/outbound mail,
automated reports and ambiguous aliases/forwards/third parties do not establish
a reply. Incomplete/unreadable evidence remains unknown. A complete no-match
check creates Waiting as of the displayed time; a match moves to local **Sent
replies**, not the entire provider Sent folder.

While Outlook Inbox is visible and no editor is open, the implementation checks
at most **two eligible current sent replies per minute**, sequentially. Hiding
Inbox, opening an editor, switching owner/mailbox or losing access stops/aborts
work. Old or cleared receipts are not automatically backfilled; explicit checks
can recover evidence after the same mailbox reconnects. There is no monitoring
while NavoX is closed. The quiet-check, scope and privacy regressions passed the recorded
release gates. Gmail's selected-thread watch contract remains separate;
refreshing its saved watch list alone does not check Gmail or send a reply.

See [provider qualification](m2-provider-qualification.md) for exact consent,
retention/cleanup and real-provider cases. The seven-day availability policy is
not a claim of a background purge occurring at the exact expiry instant.

## Recorded study streak versus actual focus

The implemented Noa streak credits a student-local day with at least one block
recorded **Done with at least 15 recorded minutes** and a valid outcome time.
Shorter blocks do not combine into a qualifying block. Task completion without
study credit does not create a streak. See [implementation](../../services/api/navox/api/noa.py)
and [tests](../../services/api/tests/test_noa_streak.py).

**Actual elapsed focus tracking is not implemented.** The `actual_minutes`
record is not a timer or proof of uninterrupted work. The accepted Part 2 rule
requires at least 15 real elapsed focus minutes, including a qualifying early
stop. Focus timing, pause/freeze behavior, approved mood/state interactions and
data-driven rewards remain Part 2 under the [accepted decisions](vox-m1-decisions.md).
The recorded-study streak does not complete those requirements.

## Fresh-account five-step entry

The five source-backed entry points are Classes, calendar Connections, email
Connections, Planner task capture and Vox. The fresh-account test must record
that each opens its correct first-time flow, without inserting sample work or
fabricating a connection just to turn steps green. **Entry-test result:
Passed: all five setup destinations, anonymous entry and saved-timezone checks; saved fresh-account state unchanged. Provider consent and live speech were not attempted in this read-only run..** Record its account state and deployed revision.

An entry-navigation pass does not establish all five completion outcomes.
Calendar/email remain incomplete without their required grants. Try Vox requires
the owner-verified call to be active and speaking; a modal or standby state is
insufficient. A read-only test that stops before authorization/call admission
must say so. Real consent, mailbox reads and real Vox evidence remain separate.
