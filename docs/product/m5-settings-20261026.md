## Vox Forget review handoff

The authenticated current agent can publish at most four metadata matches on the dedicated memory-review topic. The browser validates the topic payload under the existing exact-agent/lease fence and clears it on session suspension or call completion. It never deletes or navigates automatically. Review in Settings carries a five-minute, owner-bound, one-use search draft and preserves the app-owned call while minimizing it. The fixed route omits the private query. Settings reloads current facts and history and requires the existing explicit deletion confirmation. A fact correction becomes current memory while preserving historical conversation text. Source-linked fact deletion explicitly discloses source and derivative erasure.

The handoff adds no voice deletion authority. Labeled component captures are visual evidence only; they do not establish live-provider or physical-phone acceptance.
